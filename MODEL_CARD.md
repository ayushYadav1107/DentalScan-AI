# Model card — DentalScan AI detector

## Model details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Architecture** | YOLOv10s (also trained: YOLOv8n, YOLOv12m) |
| **Task** | Object detection, 6 classes, single-label per box |
| **Input** | RGB image, resized to 640×640. Source images are greyscale panoramic radiographs (OPG) |
| **Output** | Bounding boxes with class and confidence |
| **Initialisation** | COCO-pretrained backbone, fully fine-tuned |
| **Schedule** | 250 epochs, batch 8, SGD/auto optimizer, lr0 0.01, cosine off, mosaic closed for the last 10 epochs |
| **Augmentation** | Mosaic 1.0, MixUp 0.2, copy-paste 0.1, HSV (0.015 / 0.7 / 0.4), rotation ±10°, shear 5°, scale 0.5, translate 0.1, hflip 0.5, vflip 0.2 |
| **Training time** | 72 min on a single consumer GPU |
| **Weights** | `runs/detect/Yolo_10s_train/weights/best.pt` (16 MB) |

## Intended use

**In scope.** Research and education. Demonstrating detection on dental
radiographs. A starting point for work on small-dataset medical detection.

**Out of scope.** Any clinical decision, screening programme, triage queue, or
patient-facing output. The model has not been validated against clinical
outcomes, its confidences are not calibrated probabilities, and it has been
evaluated on 23 radiographs from one source.

**Explicitly not.** A diagnostic device. It is not regulated, not cleared, and
not suitable for use on real patients.

## Training data

[Dental OPG X-Ray Dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/c4hhrkxytw/4),
Mendeley Data — 231 annotated panoramic radiographs. The public release ships a
split with the training portion offline-augmented 3×.

| Class | Train boxes | Val boxes | Test boxes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Healthy | 3293 | 67 | 54 |
| Caries | 853 | 14 | 26 |
| Impacted | 729 | 18 | 12 |
| Fractured | 526 | 9 | 5 |
| Infection | 172 | 4 | 5 |
| BDC/BDR | 69 | 3 | **0** |

Training class imbalance: **48:1**. No source radiograph appears in more than
one split (verified; `dataset.py audit`).

**What is not known about this data.** Acquisition site, machine models,
patient demographics, age distribution, and geography are all undocumented in
the release. Annotation was performed once with no reported inter-rater
agreement, so label noise cannot be distinguished from model error.

## Performance

On the 23-image validation split, at confidence 0.25:

| Class | N | AP@0.5 | Recall | 95% interval on recall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Impacted | 18 | 0.995 | 1.00 | [0.82, 1.00] |
| Caries | 14 | 0.849 | 0.79 | [0.52, 0.92] |
| Healthy | 67 | 0.837 | 0.64 | [0.52, 0.75] |
| Fractured | 9 \* | 0.995 | 0.89 | [0.56, 0.98] |
| Infection | 4 \* | 0.870 | 0.50 | [0.15, 0.85] |
| BDC/BDR | 3 \* | 0.995 | 1.00 | [0.44, 1.00] |

\* fewer than 10 instances — reported for completeness, not a reliable estimate.

**Aggregate mAP@0.5 is 0.923 over all six classes and 0.894 over the three with
adequate support.** Quote the second, or quote neither.

## Failure modes

- **Missed detections dominate.** Inter-class confusion is ~1% of predictions;
  essentially all error is ground truth assigned to background.
- **`Infection` recall is 0.50.** Half of periapical radiolucencies are never
  proposed. This is the most clinically consequential failure in the model.
- **`Healthy` recall is 0.64**, and 82% of background false positives are
  labelled `Healthy` — the detector treats many unremarkable tooth regions as
  background and hallucinates `Healthy` boxes elsewhere.
- **`BDC/BDR` is effectively unmeasured.** 69 training instances, 3 validation,
  0 test.
- **Confidence is uncalibrated.** A displayed 0.80 does not mean 80% of such
  detections are correct. No calibration analysis has been run.
- **Single-source data.** Behaviour on radiographs from a different machine,
  exposure protocol, or population is unknown and should be assumed poor.

## Ethical considerations

Dental radiography is used in contexts — insurance assessment, treatment
justification, forensic age estimation — where a false positive has cost and a
false negative has clinical consequence. A model with 0.50 recall on infection
would, if trusted, miss half of the cases it was pointed at. The interface
displays confidences and saliency overlays precisely so that outputs are read as
suggestions to a human, never as findings.

The `Healthy` class is a background label, not a diagnosis. A `Healthy` box
means "this region looked unremarkable to a detector", not "this tooth is
healthy". Nothing in this system supports the second claim.
